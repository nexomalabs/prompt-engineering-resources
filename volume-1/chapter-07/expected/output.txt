Text the tokenizer was trained on compresses well. Unseen words fall
back to near one token per character, and numbers fragment badly.
This is why a prompt in a rare language can cost several times more
than the same content in English.

Same 20,000 characters. unseen words costs 4.7x more than common English,
because it needs 6.4x the tokens to say it.
At 50,000 calls a day the difference is $2,623.

Token count is not a detail. It is the unit you are billed in, and
it depends on what the tokenizer was trained on rather than on how
much you actually said.
