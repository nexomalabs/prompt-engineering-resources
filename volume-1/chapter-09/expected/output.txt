The direct condition gives an immediate, confident, wrong answer on every
problem here — it never shows work, so it never catches its own mistakes.
Chain-of-thought recovers most of them by making the model produce
intermediate steps it can be checked against, but a step can still contain
an arithmetic slip that CoT prompting alone does not catch. The reasoning
model gets every problem right, at the cost of far more tokens and latency
spent on hidden reasoning before the visible answer.
